Making Carrot Fries: A Quick Snack

Want a quick snack using ingredients you might already have at home? Try these easy carrot fries.

What you'll need

Carrots, butter, salt, pepper, and optional chili flakes and paprika.

How to make them

  1. Peel as many carrots as you'd like to eat, then cut them into long pieces shaped like French fries.
  2. Season the carrots with salt and pepper. Add chili flakes or paprika if you like.
  3. Melt a little butter in a pan over medium heat. Add the carrots and cook, stirring regularly, until they are tender and lightly browned.
